Contact

support@asanaquiz.app

If you are reporting a bug, it helps to include your device type, iOS or macOS version, app version, and a short description of what happened.

Quick Help

  • Use the Practice tab to start your next session or review missed poses.
  • Use the Library tab to browse quiz collections and asana references.
  • Use the Profile tab to change language, design style, reminders, and premium access.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I restore premium access?

Open the Profile tab and use the Restore Purchases button in the premium card.

Do I need to sign in to use the app?

No. You can use AsanaQuiz without signing in. Sign-in is optional and is mainly useful for syncing progress across devices.

Why does my progress look different after signing in?

The app merges local practice history with your signed-in account history. If something looks incorrect, contact support and include which device you used before signing in.

How do reminders work?

Reminder permission is only requested after your first completed practice. You can manage reminders later from the Profile tab.

Troubleshooting

Premium unlock is not appearing

Make sure the purchase completed successfully and try Restore Purchases from Profile. If the issue continues, contact support.

Quiz collections are not loading

Check your network connection and reopen the app. Some content may also be available locally when remote sync is unavailable.

Language did not update immediately

If a screen appears stale after changing language, return to the previous tab or reopen the affected screen. If the issue repeats consistently, report it to support.
